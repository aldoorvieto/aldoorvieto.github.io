## Discography page structure

Per the customer's request (August 2026), the discography page has two sections: the complete list (shared include, see above) and an "Integrali pianistiche / Complete Piano Works" section in `studio/discografia.md` and `studio/en/discography.md`, listing the Togni, Castiglioni, and Malipiero complete cycles with catalogue numbers. The customer listed Stradivarius STR 37368 as "Vol. 3 (questo volume esce a settembre)"; since STR 37243 is already Vol. 3, it was published as **Vol. 4, forthcoming September 2026** — confirm with Aldo. It is deliberately not yet in the complete numbered list.

## Press quotes

The quotes added to `studio/stampa.md` / `studio/en/press.md` in August 2026 were mined from the customer's private press archive (`Critiche ORVIETO` and `Lettere Elogi`, supplied via TransferNow, not kept in the repository). Every quote was transcribed via OCR and verified against the extracted text; references (author, publication, date, subject) follow the original clippings. The Fubini and Quirino Principe quotes marked as letters come from private correspondence supplied by the artist for this purpose. Online links were verified reachable in August 2026 except amadeusonline.net, which blocks automated requests.
