Throughout his career, Aldo Orvieto has taken part in hundreds of premieres and has received new works dedicated to him by Italian and European composers. This page presents a representative selection.

## Selected world premieres

### 2026 — Nicola Bernardini, *D’altro canto 2*; Andrea Mannucci, *Requiem per Giulia*

For ensemble, Chigiana International Festival, Siena. Aldo Orvieto, piano.  
[Programme](https://www.chigiana.org/pf/15-luglio-2026/)

### 2025 — Claudio Ambrosini, *Orienteering*

2019/2025 version for electronically revealed piano. Venice, Ex Novo Musica.  
[Programme](https://www.exnovoensemble.it/files/2025_12_20.shtml)

### 2022 — Nicola Sani, *Unstable terra*

For piano, geophone, live electronics, and eight-channel digital media. Commissioned by Ex Novo Musica and produced by SaMPL; Benedetto Marcello Conservatory, Venice.  
[Programme](https://www.conservatoriovenezia.eu/blog/eventi/collaborazioni/concerto-ex-novo-ensemble/)

### 2022 — Giovanni Mancuso, *Osszce Russzce Sszcey*; Alessandro Solbiati, *Corde e Martelletti Suite*

Ex Novo Musica commissions for ensemble. Teatro La Fenice, Sale Apollinee, Venice.  
[Programme](https://www.teatrolafenice.it/en/event/rethinking-childhood/)

### 2022 — Claudio Ambrosini, *Quattro Haiku*

For piano. Commissioned by Ex Novo Musica and premiered by Aldo Orvieto.  
[Programme](https://www.exnovoensemble.it/files/2022_10_11.shtml)

### 2011 — Gustav Mahler / Camillo Togni, *Adagietto* from the Fifth Symphony

World premiere of Togni’s 1973 piano transcription. Giorgio Cini Foundation, Venice.  
[Programme](https://www.cini.it/en/eventi/ex-novo-musica-stagione-2011-viii-edizione-3/)

### 2009 — Bruno Maderna, *Concerto* for piano and orchestra (1946)

World premiere of the rediscovered work.  
[Artist profile](https://www.amicimusicapadova.org/musicisti/aldo-orvieto/)

## Rediscoveries and first recordings

The first modern performance of Camillo Togni’s *Variations* for piano and orchestra, together with the complete Togni and Niccolò Castiglioni recording projects, documents a sustained engagement with sources and neglected repertoire.
