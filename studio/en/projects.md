## Ex Novo

Aldo Orvieto was among the founders of the **Ex Novo Ensemble** in Venice in 1979 and of the **Ex Novo Musica** concert series in 2004. Across more than four decades, the project has presented contemporary music through concerts, commissions, world premieres, and collaborations with composers, performers, and research centres.

[Ex Novo Ensemble](https://www.exnovoensemble.it/)

## SaMPL

His collaboration with **SaMPL — Sound and Music Processing Lab** at the Cesare Pollini Conservatory explores the relationship between instrumental performance, live electronics, sound direction, and new listening spaces. In 2019, Aldo Orvieto and Veniero Rizzardi edited a volume documenting the laboratory’s first ten years.

## Complete piano surveys

A central part of Orvieto’s recording work is devoted to the systematic recovery and documentation of twentieth-century Italian piano repertoire. His complete surveys of **Camillo Togni**, **Gian Francesco Malipiero**, and **Niccolò Castiglioni** bring together established works, rarities, and world-premiere recordings.

## Recent projects

### BUSONI100 — 2024

A project by the Cesare Pollini Conservatory and Amici della Musica di Padova examining Ferruccio Busoni’s continuing relevance as composer, performer, and thinker. Its programme combined an international conference, concerts, and advanced training; Orvieto contributed to the conference.

[BUSONI100](https://www.conservatoriopollini.it/site/it/produzione-eventi-dettaglio/id-4560-titolo-ferruccio%2Bbusoni%2B100.html)

### Padua for Gian Francesco Malipiero — 2023

Concerts, talks, film screenings, and a masterclass marked the fiftieth anniversary of Malipiero’s death. The project also presented Orvieto’s complete recording of the composer’s piano music for Stradivarius.

[Project details](https://www.amicimusicapadova.org/evento/padova-per-g-f-malipiero-3/)
