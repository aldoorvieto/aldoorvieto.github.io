Born in Padua in 1961, Aldo Orvieto studied at the Venice Conservatory and continued his musical development with Aldo Ciccolini, an important influence on his artistic formation.

Particularly associated with twentieth-century and contemporary music, he has appeared as a soloist with the RAI National Symphony Orchestra, the orchestras of Teatro La Fenice, Teatro Comunale di Bologna and Arena di Verona, the Orchestra della Toscana, and Ensemble 2e2m in Paris.

Chamber music is central to his work. His collaborators have included Luigi Alberto Bianchi, Felix Ayo, Dora Bratchkova, Luigi Piovano, Sara Mingardo, Monica Bacelli, and Luisa Castellani. He has also taken part in numerous world premieres of works by Italian and European composers.

His discography includes more than one hundred releases for labels including Naxos, Stradivarius, cpo, Mode, Kairos, and Dynamic, among them complete surveys of the piano music of Gian Francesco Malipiero, Camillo Togni, and Niccolò Castiglioni.

A founding member of the Ex Novo Ensemble in 1979 and of the Ex Novo Musica concert series in 2004, he received the Italian Music Critics’ Abbiati Prize in 2019 for a recording of chamber works by Claudio Ambrosini. In 2020, together with Luigi Piovano, he received the Isang Yun Prize.

He teaches chamber music at the Cesare Pollini Conservatory in Padua and has published essays on twentieth-century piano repertoire and German Lied.
